# Lessons Learned

## Purpose

This directory records lessons extracted from completed work. Lessons
capture what worked, what failed, and what should change so that future
tasks benefit from past experience instead of repeating mistakes.

## What Belongs Here

- Post-task reflections on what succeeded and why.
- Descriptions of failures or difficulties and their root causes.
- Recurring problems observed across multiple tasks.
- Insights that point to missing skills, rules, knowledge, or workflow
  improvements.
- Proposed framework improvements with their rationale.

## Extracting Lessons from Completed Projects

After a significant task, review it and extract lessons by asking:

- What worked? What should be repeated?
- What failed? What was the root cause?
- What was difficult and why?
- Was a rule or skill missing that would have prevented the problem?
- Was important knowledge missing that should have been available?
- What workflow step could be improved?

Record each lesson with enough context to be understood later: the
situation, what happened, the cause, and the recommended change.

## Identifying Recurring Problems

- Review past lessons when starting similar work.
- When the same problem appears in multiple lessons, treat it as a
  recurring problem that deserves a systemic fix, not another one-off
  workaround.
- A recurring problem should be addressed by removing the root cause,
  not by repeating individual patches.

## How Lessons Reveal Missing Skills, Rules, Knowledge, or Workflow Improvements

Each lesson should indicate what it implies for the framework:

- **Missing skill**: the team or agent lacked a capability that would
  have prevented the issue.
- **Missing rule**: a guideline was absent that, if followed, would have
  prevented the mistake.
- **Missing knowledge**: information was not available when needed.
- **Workflow improvement**: a process step or order change would have
  avoided the problem.

Make the connection explicit so each lesson is actionable rather than
informational.

## Reviewing Proposed Improvements

Proposed improvements should be reviewed before adoption:

- Confirm the problem is real and the evidence supports it.
- Confirm the proposed improvement addresses the root cause.
- Consider side effects on existing behavior and conventions.
- Confirm the improvement is consistent with the framework principles
  in `AGENTS.md`.

## Human Approval Requirement

Improvements that modify core framework behavior (rules, workflows,
principles, or other governing behavior defined in framework documents)
must not be applied without explicit human approval. Record the lesson
and the proposed improvement, but do not change core framework
behavior until a human has approved it.

## Handling Obsolete Lessons

- Mark a lesson as obsolete when it is superseded by newer knowledge,
  when the underlying technology or conditions change, or when it is
  proven incorrect.
- Do not silently delete lessons. Preserve the history so the reason
  for the change remains traceable.
- An obsolete lesson should record what changed and what supersedes it.
